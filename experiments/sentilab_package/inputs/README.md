# TAP-2.5D reference case for an independent FE thermal comparison

This repository holds a complete, machine-readable steady-state thermal case: the 2.5D chiplet package **as the TAP-2.5D tool models it for HotSpot 6.0**, for the two original layouts published in Ma et al., "TAP-2.5D", DATE 2021. Case A is the Huawei Ascend 910-class layout (paper Fig. 6(a)); case B is the CPU-DRAM layout (Fig. 5(a)). It is for an independent finite-element (FE) solution, compared with the paper's published block-centre temperatures.

It is the MODEL's stack, not a physical package, and not silicon or manufacturer data.

- **SL:decode**: die centres and rotations derived from Fig. 6(a) and Fig. 5(a) by the SentiLab team. Method: vector extraction from the PDF (page 6). Stroked line segments are grouped into closed rectangles. The panel scale comes from the 0.5 mm edge strips TAP draws around the 45 mm interposer. Die outlines, taken in drawing order as TAP dies 0..n-1, are checked against each die's catalogue size. A centre is the outline's midpoint in mm, with the origin at the interposer's lower-left corner. Per-coordinate uncertainty is 0.0007 to 0.0022 mm (figure reading only).
- **GEN:A/..., GEN:B/...**: the HotSpot inputs that TAP's generator writes for these layouts (TAP config + intp_size 45 + SL:decode). They were regenerated byte for byte; their sha256 values are in case.json "sources", and CASE-SCHEMA.md shows how to rebuild them.

A citation KEY:N means line N of that source at the listed commit; case.json "sources" gives each file's URL, sha256 and git blob id.

## 1. Goal

An independent 3D steady-state FE solution of -div(k grad T) = q for cases A and B. Both are fully defined: same stack, different floorplan. Report the block-centre temperatures (section 5) with a convergence study.

## 2. Geometry: the model stack, bottom to top

Origin at the interposer's lower-left corner; z up from the substrate bottom. Layers 0-5 span [0, 0.045] m in x and y. All k are isotropic and constant.

Spreader edge = 2 x interposer and sink edge = 2 x spreader, centred (TAP:passive_interposer.py:154-155; PAPER Sec. III-A, p. 2; HS6:temperature.c:428-447). Gaps between dies are underfill (TAP:util/fill_space.py:19, 164). Composites are an area-weighted parallel mix k = k_Cu/(1+A) + k_fill A/(1+A), with A = (pitch/diameter)^2 - 1, k_Cu = 400, k_underfill = 1.6, k_Si = 100 (TAP:passive_interposer.py:25-42). That gives A = 4.76 for C4 (0.6 mm / 0.25 mm), 24 for TSVs (50 um / 10 um) and 2.24 for microbumps (45 um / 25 um), applied isotropically as configured.

Simplifications to keep: one TIM, no TIM2, no lid, mould or contact resistance; the sink's fins are lumped into h; the organic substrate is only 45 mm wide and its bottom is adiabatic; uniform power per die.

## 4. Boundary conditions

- **Sink top face** (z = 0.00846 m, area 0.0324 m^2): -k dT/dn = h (T - T_amb) with T_amb = 318.15 K and h = 2777.78 W/(m^2 K). So h x A = 90 W/K = 1/r_convec, r_convec = 0.0111111 K/W = 0.1 x 0.06^2 / 0.18^2 (TAP:util/hotspot.config:17,19,78; TAP:passive_interposer.py:156). HotSpot splits r_convec over every sink node by area (HS6:temperature_grid.c:831-835; HS6:temperature.c:444-447), which is a uniform h.
- **Adiabatic**: every other external face (0.0378288 m^2 in total): substrate bottom (no secondary heat path, TAP:util/hotspot.config:48), stack sides, the spreader and sink bottom annuli and their sides.
- **Interfaces**: perfect thermal contact everywhere inside.

## 5. Target metric

The paper's number is TAP-2.5D's selector: the maximum over HotSpot's centre-mapped block temperatures of all layers on the 64x64 grid, minus 273.15 (TAP:passive_interposer.py:209-210; TAP:util/hotspot.config:112,114,123). A centre value is a grid-node mean of 1, 2 or 4 cells (HS6:temperature_grid.c:1746-1760), so it has no unique continuum point.

**FE primary (pre-declared):** T_i = T at die i's centre (table above) on the chip-layer mid-plane z = 0.000465 m, with a volumetric source; M = max_i T_i - 273.15 degC. From the same run: T_i at z = 0.00039 (die bottom) and 0.00054 m (die top), and averages over the HotSpot centre cells (listed per block in case.json). **Variant S:** die power as a flux on the die bottom face, finest mesh at least.

**Paper values:**
- Case A: **75.48 degC**. PAPER Sec. IV-C, p. 5: "the peak temperature of Ascend 910 System is 75.48oC"; layout Fig. 6(a), p. 6.
- Case B: **115.94 degC**. PAPER Sec. IV-B, p. 5: "the system operates at 115.94oC"; layout Fig. 5(a), p. 6.

Our own evaluator's numbers are not provided. Our older values were withdrawn, and our corrected evaluator has not yet passed this same reproduction, so your result is an independent comparison against the paper.

## 6. Ambiguities (configured value given; treatment pre-declared)

- **A1, die power through the thickness.** The configuration says only that layer 4 dissipates power (TAP:passive_interposer.py:148). HotSpot 6.0 links layer n to n+1 through the full resistance of layer n (HS6:temperature_grid.c:2228-2230). Each sink cell meets ambient through its own resistance plus the convection share (HS6:temperature_grid.c:831-835, 2333-2336). In 1-D that equals a continuum stack with every node on the substrate-side face of its layer, so the power effectively enters at the die bottom face (inferred). Run the primary (volumetric) and variant S.
- **A2, readout point.** grid_map_mode center gives a node value with no continuum location; read the bracket in section 5.
- **A3, convection split.** One r_convec for the whole 0.18 m sink, divided by area: a uniform h on the sink top face. There are no fins and no other cooled face.
- **A4, case A floorplan gap.** No block covers x in [0.001355, 0.0013601] m, y in [0.0152603, 0.0299403] m (5.1e-06 m x 0.01468 m) in L3 and L4. TAP's fill step skips gaps under its 1e-5 m tolerance (TAP:util/fill_space.py:27, 37, 43-47). HotSpot gives the uncovered part of a cell no conductance (HS6:temperature_grid.c:95-97, 180-182). **Pre-declared fill: underfill, k = 1.6.** A void is an optional check. Case B tiles exactly.
- **A5-A11** (details in case.json "ambiguities"): composites are isotropic as configured; the L4 edge strips carry the microbump composite (TAP:passive_interposer.py:94-97); floorplan floats are used as given; the paper's selector also covers HotSpot package nodes; the paper's solver tolerance is not recorded; the layout is decoded from a figure (SL:decode); the die sizes and powers are TAP-2.5D's assumptions, and the shipped Ascend 910's dummy dies are not modelled.

## 7. Analytic estimates (from the inputs; not solver results)

- Energy balance: the area-mean of T - T_amb on the sink top must equal P_total x r_convec = 3.8889 K (A) and 7.5556 K (B). **Analytic estimate from the inputs, not a solver result.**
- Power-placement scale: q'' t_die / k_Si of the hottest die is 0.8434 K (A, 256 W over 0.0314 m x 0.0145 m) and 3.0303 K (B, 150 W over 0.00825 m x 0.009 m), with t_die = 0.00015 m and k_Si = 100. This is the order of the A1/A2 bracket. **Analytic estimate from the inputs, not a solver result.**

## 8. Report back

Per case:
- M and its die;
- T_i at the three z, and the cell averages;
- field max and min with locations;
- energy balance: P_in, the integral of h (T - T_amb) dA over the sink top, and the relative error;
- variant S.

For at least 3 meshes: element order, elements, DOFs, M per mesh, observed order, extrapolated M and an error estimate (aim for 0.1 K or better). Also the residual ||Ku - f|| / ||f||, run time and memory; the Python, NumPy, SciPy, scikit-fem and mesher versions; and one script that rebuilds everything from case.json (record its sha256), with a SHA256 manifest. Report M minus the paper value; no verdict is needed.

## 9. What this is not

Not silicon validation, not measured or manufacturer data, not CAD. It is the configured model of a layout read from a figure. A match shows that two simulations of one model agree.
