## 1. The component

**Trunnion cap TC-35** (drawing TC-35-001, rev C). A forged and machined steel cap that closes the head end
of a hydraulic actuator and carries the actuator's two trunnion pins. The CAD model is `geometry.step`, in
metres, at true size. Dimensions below are in mm, in the CAD frame.

- **Body.** A cup along the z axis. Its outer surface is a plain cylinder of Ø34.5 mm from z ≈ 1.5 mm up to
  z ≈ 58.2 mm. The bore is Ø29.4 mm, open at the bottom (z = 0) and blind at the top: its flat end face sits at
  z = 55.1 mm, under the cap.
- **Cap.** The top of the cup is 6.1 mm thick. It ends in a flat top face (Ø28.3 mm, at z = 61.25 mm) ringed by
  a rounded bead: a toroidal round of radius about 3.1 mm, Ø37.3 mm overall, slightly wider than the body.
- **Open end.** A narrow flat annular end face at z = 0 and a 45° chamfer of about 1.5 mm on its outer edge.
- **Trunnions.** Two coaxial bosses (Ø27.6 mm) on the x axis at y = 0, z = 30.6 mm, blended into the body.
  Each boss ends in a flat annular shoulder (outer Ø27.6 mm, inner Ø15.3 mm): the −x shoulder at x = −19.5 mm
  faces −x, the +x shoulder at x = +19.5 mm faces +x. From each shoulder a trunnion pin (Ø15.3 mm, 15.3 mm long)
  runs out to a flat end face at x = ±34.85 mm.
- **Material.** 42CrMo4 (AISI 4140), quenched and tempered: E = 210 GPa, Poisson's ratio 0.30, yield strength
  650 MPa. Isotropic and linear elastic for the loads considered here.
