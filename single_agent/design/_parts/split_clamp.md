## 1. The component

**Split clamp body SC-40** (drawing SC-40-002, rev B). A machined steel clamp that grips a Ø39 mm tube and
is itself mounted through its base. The CAD model is `geometry.step`, in metres, at true size. Dimensions
below are in mm, in the CAD frame.

The part is a plate-like body 16.2 mm thick: its back face (z = −8.1 mm) and front face (z = +8.1 mm) are
large flat faces with the same outline.

- **Jaw.** A ring around a bore of Ø39.3 mm whose axis is the z axis (x = y = 0). On each side the jaw's
  outer flank is a convex cylinder of radius 27.8 mm about the same axis.
- **Prongs.** The ring is split at the top. Two prongs rise to flat top faces at y = +33.8 mm, with a gap of
  25.9 mm between them (inner flat faces at x = ±12.9 mm, outer flat faces at x = ±23.7 mm). A Ø6.7 mm
  clamping-screw hole runs along x through both prongs at y = 25.7 mm, with a hexagonal nut pocket in the +x
  prong and a counterbore in the −x prong.
- **Base block.** Below the jaw, 60.7 mm wide (x = ±30.4 mm), with a flat bottom face at y = −44.0 mm. A
  Ø6.7 mm mounting hole runs along x through the base at y = −35.9 mm, with hexagonal nut pockets near both
  ends.
- **Waist.** Where the jaw meets the base block, each side has a concave cylindrical notch of radius 8.3 mm
  (axis along z, on the base block's side face, at y ≈ −19.5 mm).
- Small rounds (R 1.35 mm) on several outer corners.
- **Material.** S355 structural steel: E = 210 GPa, Poisson's ratio 0.30, yield strength 355 MPa.
